Put today's longest homers on your site

A small live widget: the day's longest home runs, updated from MLB every time it loads, linking back to the clips. Free to use on any site, newsletter page, or blog.

Paste this

<iframe src="https://nodoubters.com/embed/" width="420" height="290" style="border:0;border-radius:8px" title="Longest MLB home runs today — No Doubters" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Preview

Options

Add these to the address, e.g. /embed/?n=10&theme=light

n=5          rows to show (1–10)
theme=light  light background
team=LAD     only one team's homers (any MLB abbreviation)
date=2026-08-23   a specific day instead of today
